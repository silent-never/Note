我们采用 NSGA-III，将基础设施规划视为一个多目标优化任务，重点关注成本最小化以及网络韧性和连通性的提升。

## NO.2 电力负荷预测与不确定性

  

**Electrical load forecasting is vital in helping power system decision-making.**  

电力负荷预测对于辅助电力系统决策至关重要。

  

**In recent years, Neural Network (NN)-based load forecasting methods have been widely applied.**  

近年来，基于神经网络（NN）的负荷预测方法得到了广泛应用。

  

**However, the uncertainties in NN-based load forecasting may reduce decision-makers’ trust in the forecasts.**  

然而，基于神经网络的负荷预测中存在的不确定性可能会降低决策者对预测结果的信任。

  

**The uncertainties can be divided into two parts: epistemic uncertainty and aleatoric uncertainty.**  

这些不确定性可以分为两部分：认知不确定性和偶然不确定性。

  

**For load forecasting, quantifying these two types of uncertainties corresponds to different scenarios and has different applications.**  

对于负荷预测而言，对这两类不确定性进行量化分别对应不同的场景，并具有不同的应用。

  

**Quantifying aleatoric uncertainty, what most probabilistic load forecasting does, can leave a margin for ordinary power grid dispatching.**  

量化偶然不确定性，也就是大多数概率负荷预测所做的工作，可以为常规电网调度预留一定裕度。

  

**Epistemic uncertainty, on the other hand, quantifies whether the models understand the data well.**  

另一方面，认知不确定性用于衡量模型是否充分理解了数据。

  

**本题关键词：** electrical load forecasting：电力负荷预测；decision-making：决策；neural network：神经网络；uncertainty：不确定性；epistemic uncertainty：认知不确定性；aleatoric uncertainty：偶然/随机不确定性；quantify：量化；probabilistic load forecasting：概率负荷预测；margin：裕度；power grid dispatching：电网调度。

## NO.3 经济成本与碳排放的多目标优化

  

**There is a discernible trade-off relationship between economic costs and carbon emission objectives.**  

经济成本目标与碳排放目标之间存在明显的权衡关系。

  

**Specifically, the solutions obtained by deterministic optimization and FARO exhibit a non-dominated characteristic, forming a Pareto front.**  

具体而言，由确定性优化和 FARO 得到的解具有非支配特性，并形成 Pareto 前沿。

  

**The optimization outcome reveals that as operational costs decrease, carbon emissions increase, highlighting the inherent conflict between the economic cost objective and the carbon emission objective.**  

优化结果表明，随着运行成本降低，碳排放反而增加，这反映出经济成本目标和碳排放目标之间固有的冲突。

  

**Consequently, decision-makers face the dilemma of sacrificing one objective to optimize the other.**  

因此，决策者面临着牺牲一个目标来优化另一个目标的两难选择。

  

**The larger the weight coefficient corresponding to the objective function, the more favorable the achieved objective values in the planning process.**  

目标函数对应的权重系数越大，在规划过程中所得到的该目标值就越有利。

  

**Therefore, decision-makers can tailor their preferences by choosing a set of weight coefficients, such as assigning a higher weight to the carbon emission objective for minimizing carbon emissions.**  

因此，决策者可以通过选择一组权重系数来调整其偏好，例如为碳排放目标赋予更高权重，以实现碳排放最小化。

  

**本题关键词：** trade-off：权衡；economic cost：经济成本；carbon emission：碳排放；deterministic optimization：确定性优化；non-dominated：非支配的；Pareto front：Pareto 前沿；optimization outcome：优化结果；objective function：目标函数；weight coefficient：权重系数；decision-maker：决策者。

## NO.4 MMC-HVDC

  

**The converter is the main element of the HVDC system.**  

换流器是高压直流输电（HVDC）系统中的主要组成部分。

  

**Among the diverse converter topologies, Modular Multilevel Converter (MMC) has received considerable attention as the most promising converter technology for HVDC transmission systems and renewable energy systems such as wind and solar power plants.**  

在各种换流器拓扑结构中，模块化多电平换流器（MMC）受到了广泛关注，并被认为是应用于 HVDC 输电系统以及风电、光伏等可再生能源系统中最有前景的换流器技术之一。

  

**The MMC consists of multiple sub-modules connected in series and parallel, which allows for the flexible control of the voltage and current levels.**  

MMC 由多个串联和并联连接的子模块组成，从而可以灵活控制电压和电流水平。

  

**Compared to other conventional converter technologies, this configuration allows for a high level of power flow control, improved efficiency, and reduced harmonic distortion.**  

与其他传统换流器技术相比，这种结构能够实现更高水平的潮流控制、更高的效率以及更低的谐波畸变。

  

**Additionally, the modular design of the MMC allows for easy maintenance and replacement of individual sub-modules, increasing the overall reliability of the system.**  

此外，MMC 的模块化设计便于单个子模块的维护和更换，从而提高系统的整体可靠性。

  

**本题关键词：** converter：换流器 / 变流器；HVDC：高压直流输电；converter topology：换流器拓扑；Modular Multilevel Converter (MMC)：模块化多电平换流器；sub-module：子模块；power flow control：潮流控制；efficiency：效率；harmonic distortion：谐波畸变；modular design：模块化设计；reliability：可靠性；renewable energy：可再生能源。

## NO.5 5G 网络故障定位

  

**There is existing fault localization schemes remain difficult for complicated 5G networks due to three critical hurdles.**  

对于复杂的 5G 网络，现有故障定位方案仍然面临三个关键难题。

  

**The first challenge is that the increased depth of the network is likely to propagate errors through the causal path from the source nodes to the root node, making accurate root cause attribution difficult.**  

第一个挑战是，随着网络深度增加，错误很可能沿着从源节点到根节点的因果路径传播，从而使准确进行根本原因归因变得困难。

  

**The second challenge is the lack of adequate known labels.**  

第二个挑战是缺乏足够的已知标签。

  

**One usually has to dive deep into KPIs, service logs, and communication details before narrowing down the possible root cause candidates.**  

通常需要深入分析关键绩效指标（KPI）、业务日志以及通信细节，才能缩小可能的根本原因候选范围。

  

**Last but not least, the time-series data associated with each network node are multivariate.**  

最后同样重要的是，每个网络节点对应的时间序列数据都是多变量的。

  

**They are often in complex patterns with inter-dependence and noises, resulting in difficulty extracting node relationships.**  

这些数据通常具有复杂的相互依赖模式，并伴随噪声，从而使节点关系的提取变得困难。

  

**本题关键词：** fault localization：故障定位；causal path：因果路径；source node：源节点；root node：根节点；root cause：根本原因；attribution：归因；KPI：关键绩效指标；service log：业务日志；time-series data：时间序列数据；multivariate：多变量的；inter-dependence：相互依赖；noise：噪声。

## NO.7 可再生能源 + BESS

  

**The primary challenge of RES is its inherent intermittency, which doesn’t always align with energy demands.**  

可再生能源（RES）的主要挑战在于其固有的间歇性，这种间歇性并不总能与能源需求相匹配。

  

**One potential solution to deal with intermittency from RES is the integration of energy storage systems (ESS) with the PG, particularly battery energy storage systems (BESS) are well suited for this application.**  

解决可再生能源间歇性问题的一种潜在方法，是将储能系统（ESS）接入电网（PG），其中电池储能系统（BESS）尤其适合这一应用。

  

**These systems store excess energy produced by the RES for later use, but they can also do various things such as compensate the power factor or increase the PG power quality.**  

这些系统可以储存可再生能源产生的多余能量供后续使用，同时还能够实现功率因数补偿或提高电网电能质量等功能。

  

**Today, there are many different topologies to achieve this interface through a series of bi-directional power electronic converters to manage the power conversion effectively.**  

目前，可以通过多种不同拓扑结构实现这种接口，其中采用一系列双向电力电子变换器来有效管理功率变换过程。

  

**These converters must ensure high power quality from the PG side and safe battery operation from the BESS side, preserving the battery’s state of health (SoH) and longevity.**  

这些变换器必须保证电网侧具有较高的电能质量，同时保证 BESS 侧电池安全运行，从而维持电池的健康状态（SoH）并延长使用寿命。

  

**本题关键词：** RES：可再生能源；intermittency：间歇性；ESS：储能系统；excess energy：多余能量；power factor：功率因数；power quality：电能质量；bi-directional converter：双向变换器；power conversion：功率变换；state of health (SoH)：健康状态；longevity：使用寿命。

## NO.8 配电网故障定位

  

**This paper proposes an improved fault location method based on the three-phase current that install fault detection devices on the distribution network line.**  

本文提出了一种基于三相电流的改进故障定位方法，该方法在配电网线路上安装故障检测装置。

  

**And get the faulty component of the three-phase current in the line by subtracting the phase current before the failure occurs from the current after failure to eliminate the problem that the load current is too large to judge the weak signal.**  

通过用故障发生后的电流减去故障发生前的相电流，得到线路三相电流的故障分量，从而解决由于负荷电流过大而难以判断微弱故障信号的问题。

  

**And then the zero sequence current of the line is subtracted from the fault component of each line to enhance the difference between each line.**  

随后，将线路的零序电流从各线路的故障分量中减去，以增强不同线路之间的差异。

  

**And the variance of the fundamental wave amplitude of the three-phase difference result is calculated, so that the fault characteristics of the fault path are significantly different from the healthy path.**  

然后计算三相差值结果的基波幅值方差，使故障路径的故障特征与正常路径之间呈现明显差异。

  

**This method is not highly dependent on signal communication.**  

这种方法对信号通信的依赖程度较低。

  

**It can not only upload the signal to the master station for centralized fault location, but also indicate the fault locally to realize fault location with no signal communication.**  

它不仅可以将信号上传至主站进行集中式故障定位，也可以在本地指示故障，从而在没有信号通信的情况下实现故障定位。

  

**Moreover, it only uses the current as the judgment signal, so the method has strong applicability.**  

此外，该方法仅使用电流作为判断信号，因此具有较强的适用性。

  

**本题关键词：** three-phase current：三相电流；distribution network：配电网；fault detection device：故障检测装置；faulty/fault component：故障分量；phase current：相电流；zero-sequence current：零序电流；fundamental wave：基波；amplitude：幅值；variance：方差；fault path：故障路径；healthy path：正常路径；master station：主站；applicability：适用性。

## NO.9 智能电网 SVC 网络攻击

  

**In a smart grid, voltage control and reactive power control are crucial to its safe and reliable operation.**  

在智能电网中，电压控制和无功功率控制对于系统安全可靠运行至关重要。

  

**Static Var compensators (SVCs) are widely used to achieve these controls in a transmission system, either through centralized control by Energy Management Systems or through local closed loop control systems.**  

静止无功补偿器（SVC）被广泛应用于输电系统中实现这些控制，其控制方式可以是由能量管理系统实施集中控制，也可以采用本地闭环控制系统。

  

**In smart grids, the command channels to control SVCs are vulnerable to cyber attacks.**  

在智能电网中，用于控制 SVC 的指令通道容易受到网络攻击。

  

**In this paper, attack scenarios involving injection of malicious commands to SVCs are studied in detail.**  

本文详细研究了向 SVC 注入恶意指令的攻击场景。

  

**Attack models are established for both methods of control.**  

针对这两种控制方式分别建立了攻击模型。

  

**Based on these attack models, two detection algorithms are proposed.**  

在这些攻击模型的基础上，提出了两种检测算法。

  

**The principle behind these algorithms involves the notion that even though an adversary can manipulate the commands and measurements related to SVCs, it is nearly impossible to hide the effect on other state variables and measurements in the system.**  

这些算法背后的基本思想是，即使攻击者能够操纵与 SVC 有关的指令和测量数据，也几乎不可能隐藏这种攻击对系统中其他状态变量和测量量产生的影响。

  

**本题关键词：** reactive power：无功功率；voltage control：电压控制；SVC：静止无功补偿器；transmission system：输电系统；centralized control：集中控制；closed-loop control：闭环控制；cyber attack：网络攻击；malicious command：恶意指令；attack model：攻击模型；detection algorithm：检测算法；adversary：攻击者；state variable：状态变量；measurement：测量量。

## NO.12 电动汽车双向充电

  

**The continuous rise in the adoption of EVs has a tremendous potential to curb greenhouse gas emissions from the transportation sector.**  

电动汽车（EV）普及率的持续提高具有显著降低交通运输领域温室气体排放的潜力。

  

**EVs are now more frequently considered a wide range of distributed energy in the utility grid.**  

如今，电动汽车越来越多地被视为公用电网中的一种分布式能源资源。

  

**Hence rather than confining the EVs only to uni-directional charging operation, the EV battery storage units are now exposed to bi-directional charging to address various grid power quality issues.**  

因此，电动汽车不再仅局限于单向充电运行，其电池储能单元如今开始采用双向充电，以解决各种电网电能质量问题。

## NO.17 单相接地故障定位

  

**When single-phase earth fault occurs in the distribution network, the three-phase fault path current is different from the non-fault path current that can be used for line selection.**  

当配电网发生单相接地故障时，故障路径的三相电流与非故障路径的电流存在差异，因此可以利用这种差异进行故障选线。

  

**However, the fault component signal of the three-phase current is not obvious, because the three-phase load current is far greater than the fault component current that will lead to the failure of the method for detection of earth fault.**  

然而，三相电流中的故障分量信号并不明显，这是因为三相负荷电流远大于故障分量电流，从而可能导致接地故障检测方法失效。

  

**Improved fault location method based on the three-phase line current is proposed in this paper.**  

本文提出了一种基于三相线路电流的改进故障定位方法。

  

**The faulty component of the three-phase current is obtained by subtracting the value of phase current before failure from the value of current that appears after failure,**  

通过用故障发生后的电流值减去故障发生前的相电流值，得到三相电流的故障分量，

  

**and then the zero sequence line current is subtracted from the faulty component of each line to enhance the difference of line currents.**  

随后，再从各线路的故障分量中减去零序线路电流，以增强不同线路电流之间的差异。

  

**本题关键词：** single-phase earth fault 单相接地故障；fault path 故障路径；non-fault path 非故障路径；line selection 故障选线；fault component 故障分量；load current 负荷电流；fault location 故障定位；zero-sequence current 零序电流。
